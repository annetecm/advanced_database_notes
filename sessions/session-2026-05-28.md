# Session – 2026-05-28

## Topics covered
- OLAP: Information technology designed to query and analyze large volumes of historical data from multiple sources
- OLTP: design and use of optimized operational databases to quickly manage, insert and modify large volumes of daily transactions in real time.

## What I understood
- OLTP are used to manage daily business operations and transactions, such as inserting, updating, or deleting records in real time. They are optimized for fast and efficient processing of many small queries, making them common in applications like banking, online stores, and reservation systems. On the other hand, OLAP systems are designed for analyzing large amounts of data to support reporting and business decisions. OLAP focuses on complex queries, aggregations, and historical data analysis, which makes it useful for dashboards, trends, and business intelligence. The main difference is that OLTP handles operational transactions, while OLAP is used for data analysis and insights.
